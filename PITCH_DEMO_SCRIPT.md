### 🎯 Key Presentation Tips for Presenters:
1. **Pacing**: Speak at a steady, confident rate (~140 words/minute).
2. **Visual Focus**: Ensure browser zoom is set to **100% or 110%** for maximum legibility of charts and metric badges.
3. **Pre-seed Demo Data**: Run `python seed_data.py` beforehand so Tab 2 and Tab 3 have rich, non-empty initial states.
